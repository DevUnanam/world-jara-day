48th coronation ceremony

Mother Ilya Anansa takes the spotlight at World Gyration.

This marks the coronation of the 48th Chief of Mother Ilya Anansa, University of Cross River State, Calabar Campus, one of the most archival Ilyasis in the Southern region of Nigeria.

The coronation will take place during the 2026 World Gyration Ceremony, bringing together members from tertiary institutions around the world for a night of culture, brotherhood, and earthquake gyration.
